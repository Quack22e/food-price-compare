Other ways to get DoorDash, Uber Eats and Grubhub data

Add prices you collected

Imported prices are checked against the same rules and labelled “Imported”. Nothing you add leaves this page.

Type prices you saw in the DoorDash or Uber Eats app

This is how DoorDash prices get in without any scraping. Open the store in the app or website as you normally would, choose Delivery or Pickup, and type what it shows. Entries are checked like any import and labelled “Imported”.

Import JSON or CSV

Accepts the full app’s export, the capture bookmarklet’s output, or CSV with the documented columns. US stores, USD and https store links on doordash.com or ubereats.com only.

Capture a store page in your own browser

DoorDash store pages show servers a bot check, so they cannot be read from here. In your own browser, open a DoorDash or Uber Eats store page, choose Delivery or Pickup, then run this bookmarklet. It reads only the menu data the page publishes (schema.org), shows it to you, and gives you JSON to paste on the left. It sends nothing anywhere. DoorDash’s terms forbid copying its site with scripts, even ones you run yourself, and Uber’s terms restrict scraping. Use it only if you accept that risk.

Capture menu

Drag “Capture menu” to your bookmarks bar, or copy the code and save it as a new bookmark’s URL. Prices you capture can depend on your login and delivery address, and are labelled as captured in your session.

Prices are item subtotals before tax, fees, tip and promotions, as published on public store pages to an anonymous visitor with no delivery address. A match is “verified” only when chain, product, size, flavor, piece count, modifiers and quantity all agree. Results name the number of locations checked; they are not a claim about every US branch.

A distant branch’s price does not mean it delivers to you. This page stores nothing and sends nothing; it ranks a published snapshot plus anything you import.
